## Proven and not proven

**Proven on simulated vendors:** scoring and its reproduction, play matching and the comparison group, research and the buying committee, drafting with the copy checks, approvals and autonomy stages, the account calendar and send windows, mailbox and domain health, the database trigger behind every send, stop-first replies, reply cleaning and rules in five languages, meetings, attribution, the statistics (Wilson and Newcombe checked against published values), signed webhooks, one-click unsubscribe, erasure, the job queue and its reaper, the monitors.

**Not proven:**

- Every AI task has an eval (315 cases, 241 written by an agent that never saw the code). Replies, suggested replies, call notes and themes run live on 2026-11-11; drafting, briefs and suggested plays re-recorded live on 2026-11-12, with no case held out of the floors: replies 99% accepted (147 of 148), drafts 88% pass after one rewrite (44 of 50) and 84% judge-faithful (42 of 50), briefs 100% faithful (20 of 20), suggested replies 63% pass every check (19 of 30) with 1 of 30 judged invented, call notes 87% recall (40 of 46), themes 89% of pairs grouped as a person would but only 58% of pairs that belong together kept together, suggested plays 11 of 15 pass every check. Known gap: suggested plays keep personas within the case in only 3 of 15, and no floor covers persona fit (`evals/DRAFT_PLAY.md`). Not a real buyer among them.
- Vendors: the DNS check is live (SPF, DKIM and DMARC over DNS-over-HTTPS) and has run against the real service. The Google Postmaster Tools adapter is live code (decision 49) but has never called Google and needs the operator's OAuth credentials. Warm-up is a simulated vendor slot (decision 50). The CRM has one live adapter, CRM Desk (`CRM=crmdesk`, decision 61), tested only against a CRM Desk holding invented data; there is no Salesforce adapter. Every other vendor is a contract only, simulated in the demo: people search, finders, verifier, catch-all resolver, company data, phone, research, mail provider, inbox tests, Slack. In live mode a vendor without a key refuses instead of simulating. Yahoo's feedback loop and Microsoft SNDS are not built.
- The HeyReach webhook has not seen a real event.
- No mail provider adapter: a live desk cannot send email yet.
- Real login only through Cloudflare Access token verification, which has not been run against a real Access app.
- Region rules are a starting point. Counsel to confirm.
- Contention from several servers at once (one process was measured).
